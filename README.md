# Skyway Stunts

Elevated track stunt racing in the browser. Race a single AI opponent along
roller-coaster circuits with no side barriers: hit the ramps at the right
speed, land without wrecking your car, and don't fall off the edge.

Play: https://torebjastad.github.io/Stuntcar-racer/

## How to play

- Win races to climb the league from Division 4 to Division 1, then take on the Super League.
- Practice mode lets you drive any track without affecting league points.
- Each hard landing or collision adds chassis damage. A car that cracks through the damage bar is wrecked.
- Boost is limited per race, so spend it on ramps and the final straight.

## Controls

**Touch (landscape):** left/right buttons steer, ACCEL to go, BOOST for turbo,
BRK to slow, and the II button to pause.

**Keyboard:**

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Left / Right, A / D, or Z / X | Steer |
| Up or K | Accelerate |
| Down or L | Brake |
| Space, Enter, or Left Shift | Boost |
| P or Escape | Pause |
